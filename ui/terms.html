What Kab Tak? does

Kab Tak? forecasts your business's cash. It plays out 1,000 possible next 90 days from your invoices, bills and bank position, shows the weeks where cash may run short and how likely that is, and compares options.

It is not advice

  • Kab Tak? computes and compares options. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and you make every decision.
  • Forecasts are estimates, not promises. They are only as good as the data you give. With little or no history, Kab Tak? uses standard assumptions (for example, that buyers pay in about 45 days), so its forecasts can differ more from what really happens.
  • Check anything important with your CA or bank before you act on it.

Your data

  • We store what you give us (your name, business name, WhatsApp number, uploaded workbook, and invoices and bills you send) only to run your forecasts.
  • Your data is kept separate from every other business. Your WhatsApp number can see only your own business.
  • To understand your messages and write plain-language summaries, your text, your voice notes, your buyer names and the forecast's headline numbers are sent to speech-to-text and language-model providers (OpenRouter and Groq). Your full ledger is not sent, and they never compute the numbers.
  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. You can ask the Kab Tak? team to delete your business and its data at any time. We process personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Using the service

  • Use your own WhatsApp number and your own business's data.
  • This is a prototype. It may change, pause or lose data without notice, so keep your own records.
  • To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses from decisions made using Kab Tak?.
