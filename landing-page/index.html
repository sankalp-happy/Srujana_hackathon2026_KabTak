From one question to the cheapest fix.

You ask the way you'd ask your CA. Four steps later you have one answer and one thing to do, and the working is there for your CA to check.

  1. १STEP 1

    Ask in your own words.

    Send a voice note on WhatsApp in Hindi, Marathi or English. "Can I take this order?" "What if my biggest buyer pays a month late?" No forms, no dashboard.

    The language model only turns your words into a question. It never produces a number.

    0:06

    Heard: new buyer, ₹12 lakh order, 90-day terms.

  2. २STEP 2

    Every invoice gets a range, not a due date.

    Buyers rarely pay on the day. Kab Tak? learns when each buyer actually pays, then asks the question in its name: kab tak? How many days late can each buyer be before you run short?

    Dots show an illustrative spread of payment days. The yellow limits come from the engine.

  3. ३STEP 3

    1,000 futures, checked week by week.

    Each of the 1,000 runs mixes those payment ranges with your wages, EMIs, GST and supplier bills. Every week gets one number: the chance you run short. The limit is 5%.

    The worst week here is 14 Dec: advance tax, GST and the GST on the new order all land together.

  4. ४STEP 4

    The cheapest fix, priced in rupees.

    Discount an invoice on TReDS, draw more on the overdraft, pay a supplier later, or send a late buyer an MSMED heads-up. Kab Tak? finds the cheapest mix that gets the risk under 5%. You decide.
