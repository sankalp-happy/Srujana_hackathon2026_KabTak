# SRUJANA TECH FEST 2026

## Rules and Guidelines

**48-Hour Software Hackathon | 24 to 26 October 2026**

Welcome to Srujana Tech Fest 2026! This document outlines the rules, expectations, facilities, submission requirements, and evaluation process for all participating teams. Please read it carefully and follow the instructions shared by the organizers throughout the event.

## 1. Event Details

- **Dates:** 24 to 26 October 2026
- **Reporting time:** 9:00 AM on 24 October
- **Reporting venue:** Registration Desk, Academic Block 2, Chanakya University
- **Duration:** 48 hours from the official start announced by the organizers
- **Themes:** Finance and Legal Services
- **Team size:** 2 to 4 members
- **Eligibility:** Participants from any institution, subject to registration confirmation.

Please report on time and complete the registration process before proceeding to your assigned location.

## 2. What to Bring

Every participanting team should bring their own:

- Laptops
- Laptop chargers
- College ID
- Extension box

Each team will be provided with **one power outlet**. Bring an extension box to connect your equipment safely and plan your workspace accordingly. Avoid overloading electrical outlets.

## 3. Participation and Team Rules

1. Teams must have between 2 and 4 registered members.
2. Participants are expected to remain engaged throughout the hackathon.
3. Team members should collaborate respectfully and contribute to the project.
4. Any changes to team composition must be approved by the organizers.
5. Follow instructions provided by the organizers and volunteers.

## 4. Problem Statements and Project Development Rules

1. The organizers will provide **6 problem statements for each theme**, Finance and Legal Services.
2. Each team must choose **one problem statement from the list provided by the organizers**.
3. Custom problem statements are not permitted. Teams must develop their solutions based on one of the official problem statements.
4. Existing libraries, frameworks, APIs, and development tools are allowed.
5. Submitting a pre-built project as the solution is not permitted. The project must be developed during the hackathon.
6. Internet access and publicly available resources are allowed.
7. AI tools are permitted. Teams must disclose how AI tools were used and should be able to explain the code, design decisions, and outputs in their project.
8. Teams must not present another person's work as their own. External code, datasets, APIs, and other significant dependencies should be acknowledged where applicable.
9. Teams should be prepared to explain which parts they developed, which resources they used, and what remains incomplete.

Using a particular technology, framework, or AI tool does not automatically earn additional marks. Evaluation is based on the quality and evidence of the completed solution.

## 5. Submission Requirements and Deadlines

All deadlines are calculated from the **official start of the hackathon**, as announced by the organizers.

### A. Demo video submission

**Deadline: 36 hours after the official start**

Every team must submit a short demo video showcasing its project.

The video should clearly demonstrate the core functionality, explain the problem being addressed, and show the solution in action. Teams should ensure that the video is accessible and that the demonstration is easy to understand.

The demo video will be used to shortlist teams for the final judging round.

### B. Complete project submission

**Deadline: 48 hours after the official start**

Every team must submit its complete project, including:

1. **Source code repository:** The code required to inspect and understand the project.
2. **README or documentation:** Project overview, setup instructions, dependencies, technical decisions, and known limitations.
3. **Working demo:** A functional demonstration of the implemented solution.
4. **Supporting files:** Any files required to run or evaluate the project.

Ensure that the repository and documentation are complete and accessible before the deadline.

The submission platform, repository access requirements, file formats, and exact submission instructions will be announced by the organizers.

Teams are responsible for ensuring that their submissions work as intended and can be accessed by the evaluation team.

## 6. Evaluations and Final Judging

### Progress evaluations

Evaluations will take place approximately every 12 hours.

During these evaluations, teams should be ready to explain their progress, demonstrate working features, and discuss technical or domain-related challenges. Keep the project in a demonstrable state throughout the event.

### Finalist selection

**Ten teams from each theme will be shortlisted**, based on their submitted demo videos.

This means up to **20 teams in total** will proceed to the final judging round, with 10 finalists from Finance and 10 from Legal Services.

### Final judging round

Shortlisted teams will participate in a final round consisting of:

1. A live presentation of the project to the jury.
2. A question and answer session with the jury.

Finalists should be prepared to explain the problem, demonstrate their solution, discuss technical decisions, justify domain assumptions, and answer questions about their implementation.

The final presentation schedule and any time limits will be announced by the organizers.

### Mentorship

Mentors may be approached for guidance, subject to their availability. Teams remain responsible for their implementation and final decisions.

## 7. Judging Criteria

All projects will be evaluated using the same 100-point rubric.

| Criterion                               |  Weight |
| --------------------------------------- | ------: |
| Problem understanding and relevance     |      15 |
| Technical depth and implementation      |      20 |
| Functional completeness and reliability |      15 |
| Domain analysis and correctness         |      20 |
| Validation and robustness               |      10 |
| User experience and accessibility       |      10 |
| Originality and presentation            |      10 |
| **Total**                               | **100** |

Judges will evaluate the actual work demonstrated by each team. They may consider:

- How well the solution addresses the assigned problem.
- The quality of implementation and technical decisions.
- Whether the core functionality works reliably.
- The correctness of financial or legal concepts used.
- Testing, validation, assumptions, and handling of edge cases.
- User experience, accessibility, and clarity of presentation.
- Originality and the team's ability to explain its work.

For Finance and Legal Services projects, teams should be transparent about limitations and avoid presenting automated outputs as guaranteed financial or legal advice.

Complex architecture, AI, blockchain, cybersecurity features, or the use of a particular framework do not earn automatic bonus marks. Their value depends on how appropriately and effectively they are used.

## 8. Accommodation and Facilities

- Beds will be available in designated sleeping areas.
- Participants who wish to continue working overnight may remain at their assigned tables.
- Snacks will be provided during the night.
- Each team will have access to one power outlet.
- Participants should keep their work areas clean and respect university property.
- Follow the instructions provided by organizers and volunteers regarding the use of rooms and facilities.

Please use designated sleeping areas for rest and keep shared workspaces orderly so that other teams can continue working.

Any additional arrangements or restrictions concerning meals, facilities, or venue access will be communicated by the organizers.

## 9. Queries and Support

Once the hackathon begins, follow this process if you need assistance:

1. Contact the volunteer assigned to your classroom.
2. Explain your issue clearly and provide any relevant details.
3. If the volunteer is unable to resolve it, politely ask them to reach out to the organizers.

Please use this support process so that issues can be handled efficiently. Avoid approaching multiple organizers for the same issue unless necessary.

## 10. Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to:

- Treat fellow participants, mentors, volunteers, judges, and organizers with respect.
- Maintain a collaborative and inclusive environment.
- Follow instructions from the organizing team.
- Respect university property and shared facilities.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, disruptive behaviour, and misuse of resources.
- Use technology, data, and external services responsibly.

Any concerns about participant conduct or event arrangements should be reported to a volunteer or organizer.

## 11. Academic Integrity and Disqualification

Plagiarism, fabricated claims about the project, deliberate misrepresentation of work, and serious misconduct are grounds for disqualification.

Teams must be honest about their implementation, the use of external resources, AI assistance, and the current state of their project.

The organizers may review suspected violations and determine appropriate action based on the circumstances and applicable event policies.

## 12. Project Ownership

Project ownership and any applicable rights to use, display, or distribute submitted work are subject to the official organizer terms.

Participants should review the applicable terms and seek clarification from the organizers if they have questions about ownership or permitted use of their work.

## 13. Final Reminders

- Arrive at the registration desk by 9:00 AM on 24 October.
- Bring your laptop, charger, college ID, and extension box.
- Submit your demo video within 36 hours of the official start.
- Submit your complete project repository and documentation within 48 hours of the official start.
- Stay updated on the official WhatsApp group for announcements and changes.
- Keep your project functional and your documentation up to date.
- Be ready to demonstrate your work and explain your decisions.
- Follow the rules, respect others, and make the most of the 48 hours.

**Important:** The submission platform, exact submission instructions, final presentation schedule, and applicable project ownership terms will be communicated by the organizers.

All participants are expected to follow the latest official instructions issued by the Srujana Tech Fest 2026 organizing team.

**Good luck, and happy building!**
